# Product

## Register

product

## Users

NEMS call-centre operators, dispatchers, and supervisors in Sierra Leone who coordinate emergency calls under time pressure. Their primary job is to capture a caller and patient quickly, follow a consistent triage protocol, determine a colour code, choose an eligible ambulance and receiving facility, and dispatch with a traceable record.

## Product Purpose

SWIFT RMS turns emergency-call intake into a structured, auditable dispatch workflow. Success means operators can move from call connection to a safe triage and ambulance decision without losing context, skipping critical questions, or switching between disconnected tools.

## Brand Personality

Operational, composed, and decisive. The interface should feel like dependable emergency infrastructure: calm enough for sustained use, urgent where patient risk demands it, and precise in every label and state.

## Anti-references

Do not make the call-centre console look like a generic analytics dashboard, a spacious consumer application, or a decorative all-dark SaaS template. Avoid hiding active-call context behind modals, relying on colour alone for urgency, or presenting ornamental metrics that do not help the operator complete the dispatch.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the active incident visible while the operator works.
2. Make the next safe action unmistakable.
3. Convert the official protocol into guided decisions, not a memory test.
4. Show operational truth, including loading, unavailable, conflict, and stale states.
5. Preserve a clear audit trail from call intake through dispatch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support keyboard-only operation, visible focus, reduced motion, readable compact text, and text or icon labels in addition to red, yellow, and green colour coding.
